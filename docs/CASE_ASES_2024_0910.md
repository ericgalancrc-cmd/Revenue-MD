# Case Description — Claim ASES-2024-0910

**Provider:** Dr. Torres, PsyD  
**Patient:** Patient #5201  
**Payer:** ASES / Mi Salud (Puerto Rico Medicaid)  
**Date of Service:** May 8, 2024  
**Amount Billed:** $245.00  
**Outcome:** Denied

---

## Background

Dr. Torres is a licensed psychologist providing telehealth behavioral health services to Medicaid enrollees under ASES / Mi Salud in Puerto Rico. On May 8, 2024, she conducted a 60-minute individual psychotherapy session via video with Patient #5201. The session involved the presence of a family caregiver and required additional clinical management due to caregiver communication complexity — a scenario that qualifies for a billing add-on under CPT 90785 (Interactive Complexity).

The claim was submitted to ASES with the following procedure codes:

| Code | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **90837** | Individual psychotherapy, 60 minutes (53+ min) |
| **90785 GT** | Interactive complexity (add-on) — Via interactive audio and video |

The claim was denied in full. ASES returned the remittance with multiple denial reasons related to modifier misuse and missing documentation.

---

## What Went Wrong — The Four Problems

### Problem 1 — GT Modifier Applied to the Wrong Code

The **GT modifier** signals that a service was delivered via interactive audio and video telecommunications (telehealth). ASES requires GT on the *primary* service line — in this case, **90837**.

The biller applied GT to **both** 90837 and 90785. This is incorrect. 90785 is an add-on code; it inherits the telehealth context from 90837 and must not carry its own GT modifier under ASES billing rules. ASES rejected both lines because of the invalid modifier placement on the add-on.

> **In plain terms:** GT belongs on 90837 only. Putting it on 90785 caused the entire claim to reject.

---

### Problem 2 — Treatment Plan Reference Missing from the Clinical Note

ASES requires that every 90837 claim include a reference to an active treatment plan in the clinical note. Specifically, the note must contain:

- The **date the treatment plan was last updated**
- The **supervising clinician's signature** (or co-signature, if applicable)

Dr. Torres's note for this session did not contain either element. Without a valid treatment-plan reference, ASES will not reimburse 90837 regardless of the clinical content of the note.

> **In plain terms:** ASES needs proof that the session was tied to an authorized treatment plan. No reference = automatic denial.

---

### Problem 3 — Interactive Complexity (90785) Not Separately Documented

CPT 90785 cannot be billed based on the general session note alone. AMA guidelines and ASES policy require a **dedicated paragraph** in the clinical documentation that identifies the specific complexity factors present during the session. Acceptable factors include:

- A caregiver or third party present during the session
- A language barrier requiring interpreter involvement
- Safety concerns or active crisis management
- Legally complex circumstances (e.g., court-ordered treatment, custody disputes)

The note for this session described the session content but did not include a separate paragraph identifying caregiver involvement as the basis for billing 90785. ASES denied the add-on line for lack of supporting documentation.

> **In plain terms:** Billing 90785 requires writing down *why* the session was complex. The note skipped that paragraph.

---

### Problem 4 — Telehealth Platform Not Named (Warning)

ASES's telehealth provider bulletin requires that the clinical note name the specific telehealth platform used to deliver the service (e.g., Doxy.me, Zoom for Healthcare, Updox). The note did not include this information.

This issue did not cause the denial on its own, but it is a common audit trigger and could lead to recoupment requests during a post-payment review.

> **In plain terms:** ASES wants to know which platform was used. Leaving it out is a compliance risk.

---

## What Needs to Be Fixed Before Resubmission

| # | Fix | Who Acts |
|---|-----|----------|
| 1 | Remove GT from 90785 — leave it on 90837 only | Biller |
| 2 | Add treatment-plan date and clinician signature to the note | Clinician |
| 3 | Add a dedicated paragraph documenting caregiver involvement as the complexity factor for 90785 | Clinician |
| 4 | Add the telehealth platform name to the clinical note | Clinician |

All four corrections must be made before the claim is resubmitted. If only the modifier is fixed without updating the note, ASES will deny again on Problems 2 and 3.
